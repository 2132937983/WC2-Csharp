#! --density 2
# Touch screen input through the touch router at window pixels, as Android delivers it: 1136x640 window,
# 568x320 layout (scale 2), 2 px per dp: edge strips 48 px, touch slop 16 px, back after 96 px inward.
# Android's system back gesture is turned off along the side edges; this edge swipe replaces it, and
# drags, taps and holds that start at an edge still reach the game.
wait 4.5
# main menu: a left-edge swipe is back -> the exit box; back closes it
mark
swipe 6 300 400 300 0.3
wait 0.8
expectlog Edge swipe: back
expectback 1
shot E1_exit_box
back
wait 1
expectback 2
# a short swipe from the edge is abandoned
swipe 6 300 70 300 0.3
wait 0.8
expectback 2
# into a conquest with taps at window pixels (logical x 2); the start button lies in the right edge strip
tap 1020 304
wait 2.5
tap 340 130
wait 5
tap 1104 604
wait 6
tap 674 544
wait 2
# a drag that starts at the left edge and runs along it moves the map
camera mark
swipe 10 500 14 140 0.6
wait 1.2
camera moved
expectback 2
# a drag in the middle moves the map
camera mark
swipe 700 300 520 380 0.4
wait 1.2
camera moved
# a left-edge swipe is back -> the pause box; the map stays where it is
camera mark
mark
swipe 4 320 500 330 0.3
wait 1
expectlog Edge swipe: back
expectback 3
camera still
shot E2_pause
# the same from the right edge: back again closes the pause box
swipe 1132 320 700 320 0.3
wait 1
expectback 4
shot E3_after
# the card button lies in the left edge strip: a tap there still works (handed over at release)
mark
tap 30 600
wait 1.5
expectlog Button clicked
shot E4_cards
exit
